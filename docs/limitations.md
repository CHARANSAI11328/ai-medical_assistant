# MediScope AI Limitations & Ethical Considerations

## Technical & Clinical Limitations
1. **Research Prototype Notice:** MediScope AI is designed strictly for research, educational exploration, and technology demonstration during hackathon evaluation.
2. **Scan Modality Boundaries:** The system supports front-facing chest radiographs, retinal fundus photographs, bone radiographs, and crop leaves. It does not process 3D DICOM volumes, CT slices, or MRI sequences in this prototype version.
3. **No Definitive Diagnosis:** Model predictions carry uncertainty. A confidence score of 85% does not mean an 85% clinical certainty of disease. Clinical correlation by a licensed healthcare provider is mandatory.
4. **Lighting & Orientation Sensitivity:** Photography modalities (such as retinal fundus or leaf images) are sensitive to lighting, glare, and resolution degradation.
